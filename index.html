ProofGate · synthetic replay receipt

Check the evidence, not just the green build.

This small, dependency-free kit reruns two invented targets, checks their file hashes, and rejects tampered or inflated receipts.

Fresh reruns

Scope control

Reject extra targets, unfinished steps, added assumptions, and inflated pass counts.
